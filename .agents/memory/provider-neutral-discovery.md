---
name: Provider-neutral discovery
description: Content rules for Visit Malindi catalogue and enquiry journeys.
---

Visit Malindi must present experiences and accommodation as concierge-led destination guidance, not as verified provider listings. Avoid unsupported popularity, accessibility, pricing, availability, review, schedule and operator claims; invite visitors to ask WhatsApp for current details instead.

Accommodation relationship labels (“Managed by Visit Malindi”, “Visit Malindi Partner”, and “General accommodation”) describe Visit Malindi’s commercial relationship, not a quality verification. When property facts or approved media are absent, disclose that details are to be confirmed and label imagery as illustrative.

For food and nightlife, name venues only when a public source supports the venue identity, category and broad location. Treat permanent venues separately from time-sensitive events: live music, DJs and parties are concierge questions until current event details are supplied.

**Why:** The product is a frontend-only discovery and manual-concierge service. Commercial and time-sensitive details have not been approved for publication.

**How to apply:** Use factual category-level descriptions and neutral editorial language in new catalogue cards, filters, detail pages and calls to action. Only add provider-specific claims when the user supplies an approved source.
